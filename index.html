About us

A catalyst for meaningful social impact

Established in 2016, Patel Foundation Trust (Zimbabwe) was founded on the belief that lasting progress comes from investing in people, strengthening communities, and creating pathways for future generations.

We partner with communities, institutions, development organisations and stakeholders to identify critical challenges and implement practical solutions that generate measurable outcomes.

Sustainable impact is created when communities are empowered with the tools, resources, knowledge, and opportunities needed to thrive.

Our Purpose

To create lasting social value through strategic initiatives that advance education, healthcare, community development, economic empowerment, and sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To build stronger and more resilient communities where every individual has access to opportunities that enable them to achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

Patel Foundation is committed to driving positive change by:

  • Investing in community development initiatives
  • Supporting education and skills advancement
  • Improving access to health and social welfare programmes
  • Encouraging entrepreneurship and economic inclusion
  • Developing partnerships that create sustainable impact

Where we work

Our impact areas

Health initiatives

  • Eye clinic: 730 screenings and 259 spectacles for employees and their families in Redcliff.
  • Dental camps: 166 extractions, 17 fillings and 60 referrals at Chivi District Hospital.
  • Stop the Bleed: trauma training and equipment for GZU, nursing schools and Masvingo General Hospital.
  • Medical oxygen: supplied to hospitals during national shortages.

Education

  • Bursaries: around 60 bursaries from primary school through university for orphaned and disadvantaged children.
  • Chisumbanje school, Chiredzi: classrooms built and furnished, a borehole drilled, and electrification under way.
  • Scholarship partnership: national reach through the Cephas Msipa Scholarship Trust.
Illustrative

Skills & ICT

  • SkillsMaker 2025: real-world exposure for students, nurses and medics.
  • Women in ICT: annual attachments with Harare Institute of Technology and in Redcliff.
  • Code Clubs & Experience AI: a pilot across three regions of Zimbabwe with the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
Illustrative

Community & children

  • Armani Children's Home, Kenya: dormitories, a borehole, hot water, scholarships, food and clothing.
  • Resilient communities: youth capacity building in Redcliff and with Dzikwa Trust in Norton.
  • Sustainable energy: electrical and energy skills training.

Our foundation story

From a vision to give back — to a platform for opportunity

Patel Foundation Trust was formally established in Zimbabwe in 2016 with a commitment to contribute positively to society through structured and purpose-driven initiatives.

What began as a vision to give back has grown into a platform for creating opportunity, strengthening communities and supporting initiatives that make a real difference.

Today, the Foundation continues to pursue initiatives that transform lives and create lasting social impact.
